# Compliance
## Procedure
1. Identify personal data, licensed code, or restricted assets in use.
2. Check handling against the project's stated ethical approval.
3. Flag any gap to the student for resolution.
## Rules
- Advisory only. The student and supervisor hold final responsibility.
